Core Pillars of Responsible AI

1. Accountability: You remain 100% responsible for all output, claims, and citations.

2. Verification: Never trust AI output without double-checking primary sources.

3. Transparency: Disclose AI usage openly where required by publishers or institutions.

4. Privacy: Keep confidential and unreleased data out of commercial AI systems.
